The problem

Great content deserves
to be seen everywhere.

Most creators and brands produce powerful long-form content — then watch it reach only the audience they already have. Without a short-form distribution system, the majority of your content's potential is left completely untapped.

01
Long-form alone won't grow you
Whether you're a streamer, podcaster, or brand — your long-form content reaches people who already know you. Short-form clips on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts are where new audiences discover you in 2026.
02
Doing it properly is a full-time job
Finding the best moments, editing, captioning, formatting for each platform, posting consistently — done right, it takes more time than you have. So it either doesn't happen, or happens badly.
03
Volume is where most people fall short
The algorithm rewards consistency and volume. Ten clips distributed across three platforms gives you thirty chances to reach a new audience today. One post gives you one. The gap compounds every single week.

How it works

One piece of content.
Distributed everywhere.

Clipping is the process of turning long-form content into short-form clips distributed across every platform simultaneously. We handle the entire process — from extracting the best moments to posting and reporting — while you focus on what you do best.

01
You share your content
A YouTube link, Twitch VOD, podcast recording, branded video — whatever long-form content you already produce. Nothing new required from you.
02
We set your campaign live
You set a budget and provide a brief — your guidelines, style preferences, topics to avoid. We launch your campaign to our vetted network of short-form editors immediately.
03
Clippers go to work
Our vetted clippers claim your campaign, extract the best moments, add captions, and post across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Multiple clippers working simultaneously means maximum volume and speed.
04
You pay only for verified views
Clippers earn a fixed rate per 1,000 verified organic views they generate. Clips that don't perform earn nothing — and cost you nothing extra. Pricing is discussed on your strategy call.
05
We report throughout
We send you regular performance updates throughout the campaign — views generated, clips live, top performers. You always know exactly what's happening with your budget in real time.
06
Campaign ends, we pitch the next
When your budget runs out, we deliver a full performance report. Then we pitch you the next campaign — with data from the first to inform the strategy. Better results every round.

Why Overture

Performance-based.
Zero risk.

Traditional agencies charge monthly retainers regardless of results. We built the opposite — a model where every dollar is earned by real, verified clipper performance.

01
Zero risk — pay for results only
Your budget goes into your campaign. Clippers earn only when their clips generate verified organic views. Clips that don't perform cost you nothing. No retainers, no wasted money — ever.
02
Volume no individual can match
A single editor produces a handful of clips per day. Our network deploys dozens of clippers on your campaign simultaneously — the volume, speed, and platform coverage is impossible to replicate with any solo hire or internal team.
03
Far cheaper than paid advertising
Paid ads on TikTok or Instagram average $8–15 CPM. Organic short-form distribution through Overture typically delivers views at a fraction of that cost — with authentic engagement that converts into real followers and real customers.

Consistent communication, always

Questions

Everything you
need to know.

What kind of content works with Overture?
+
Any long-form video content — YouTube videos, Twitch or Kick streams, podcast recordings, interviews, webinars. Ideally 30+ minutes long so there are multiple strong clip moments. The more content you have, the more clips we can distribute.
How does performance-based pricing work?
+
You set a campaign budget. Clippers earn a fixed RPM (rate per 1,000 verified views) for every view they generate. The campaign runs until the budget is exhausted. Full pricing details are covered on your strategy call.
How do you verify that views are real?
+
Every submission is reviewed for authenticity — engagement rates, account history, view velocity, and geographic data. Bot traffic and manipulated views are flagged and excluded. We hold all payouts for 7 days after campaign close before any money moves.
Do I control how my content is clipped?
+
Yes. Before launch you provide a brief — clip style preferences, topics to avoid, specific moments you want featured, brand guidelines. Clippers follow your guidelines as a condition of participating in the campaign.
What is the minimum budget?
+
Starter campaigns begin at €500. This gives you meaningful distribution scale to test the model and see real results before committing to larger campaigns.
How long does a campaign run?
+
Campaigns run until the budget is fully spent on verified views — there's no fixed end date. Distribution begins within 24–48 hours of launch. Throughout the campaign you receive regular performance updates, and a full report at close — total views, clips created, top performers, and platform breakdown.
How do you keep us updated during a campaign?
+
We send regular performance reports throughout every campaign — not just at the end. You'll always know how many views have been generated, which clips are performing best, and how much budget remains. After the campaign closes, we come back with a full breakdown and a proposal for the next campaign.
