THE PROCESS

You keep recording. We handle the rest.

Four steps between your raw footage and clips running in the feeds.

01 BRIEF

We set the campaign together

One call and a shared doc. Editing guide, brand rules, platforms and budget get locked in, so every editor in the network works from the same standard.

02 CUT

The network starts cutting

Editors cut your source material into clips built around strong openings. Different editors test different moments, hooks and styles at the same time, which is something a single in-house editor can never do.

03 REVIEW

Every clip gets checked

Nothing goes live without a review. Each clip is checked against your guide before posting, so your brand stays exactly the way you set it up.

04 REPORT

You see everything, weekly

Every clip, its views and what we paid editors for those views, in one weekly report. What performs gets more budget, what does not gets cut. You always know where the campaign stands.

THE MODEL

You pay for views, not for effort

Budget follows results

You set the budget. A flat management fee covers strategy, the editing guide, clipper management, review and reporting. Everything else goes to editors as payouts per thousand views their clips earn.

When a clip takes off, the editor behind it earns more and your money flows toward what works. When a clip flops, it costs you almost nothing.

Most campaigns start as a 90 day pilot. Long enough to produce real data, short enough to stay a low risk test.
